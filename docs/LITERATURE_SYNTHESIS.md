# Literature review notes matrix
A researcher-authored, reviewed notes workflow, not a thesis-writing or empirical conclusion generator. Links exact finding text to candidate evidence records and requires two supporting review attestations. Study design, population, constructs, limitations, applicability and direction labels must be explicitly supplied by the researcher. Groups by theme, reports distinct source hashes and mixed direction labels without effect pooling or vote-counting interpretation. Never automatically drafts a literature conclusion or invents results. JSON and spreadsheet-readable CSV output, with formula-prefix protection. Synthetic fixture tests only. No real study content on public GitHub. Actual authorship, source relevance, independent review and university AI disclosure remain mandatory.
