# Independent evidence review ledger
Separate source review from automatic candidate matching and Word color inspection. Each reviewer attests a decision against the exact candidate record hash, source hash, indexed physical page and crop hash. For assessed claims require actual excerpt, rationale and full-context inspection attestation. Unassessed records remain incomplete. Two distinct reviewer identifiers with agreeing decisions produce reviewer agreement, not a VERIFIED claim; disagreement requires reconciliation. Reviewers must inspect actual originals independently and validate semantic attribution, not infer it from matched words. Identity and independence are not authenticated here. No automatic acceptance or academic release; no real documents, personal identifiers or actual claims in public CI. Use synthetic fixtures for tests.
