# RU article builder — separate synthetic structure
Independent article profile; not the IS full-volume template. Official 2569 article layout has not been verified. Sample font, sizes, A4, margins and spacing are explicitly example-only, not a claim of RU requirements.
Generates labeled Word article structure with bilingual abstract placeholders, author/keyword placeholders, introduction, literature, methods, synthetic computed results, discussion/limitations/recommendations and reference placeholder. No invented authors, citations, empirical findings or actual study materials. All numeric statements originate from the deterministic academic draft; manifest retains facts and source hash. Reopens output for paragraph/style/hash and header/footer label checks. Only synthetic testing enabled; actual article template, independent academic review, Word visual inspection and institution publication rules remain required.
