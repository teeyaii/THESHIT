# Native extraction comparison pilot
Reuses the existing five fixed publisher URLs, visually transcribed first-page titles and NFC/whitespace normalization. Downloads each PDF once and compares pdfplumber against Poppler pdftotext -layout for that exact same source hash. Records per-extractor title exact match, text presence and blocked failures. No PDFs, raw page text or private content uploaded. Exact title match is a narrow diagnostic, not independent corpus CER or semantic support. Do not choose the best extractor per title and then report a combined accuracy. Full independent Thai text/table/OCR/locator benchmark remains required.
