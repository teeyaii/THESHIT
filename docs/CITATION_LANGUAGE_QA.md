# Citation and Thai editorial QA
Read-only checks on a caller-prepared independent citation span inventory bound to exact DOCX SHA-256: exact text and locations, citation-to-reference IDs, missing and orphaned references, duplicate/invalid DOI, metadata and claim review flags. No publication-age exclusion; older foundational sources are permitted. These checks do not automatically discover every citation, verify DOI existence, apply APA 7 typography or establish semantic support. Reviewer assertions must be confirmed separately.
Editorial candidates include suspicious internal Thai multi-space, zero-width characters, unfilled placeholders and unusual punctuation. No auto-edits, no AI authorship detection, no claim of Thai stylistic quality. Review legitimate exceptions against the approved text. Do not publish manuscript or reference inventory on GitHub; synthetic fixtures only.
